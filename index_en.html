      DESIGN RULES / MATHEMATICS
      
      
      Three inequalities
      that govern every system.
      
      
      
        
      
          EQ—01 / LITTLE'S LAW
          L = λ · W
          
      Queue length is arrival rate times service time. Want a shorter queue? Cut W — don't just add workers.
      
        
      
        
      
          EQ—02 / AMDAHL
          S = 1 / (1 − p + p/n)
          
      The serial fraction caps every parallel effort. Measure p before you scale n.
      
        
      
        
      
          EQ—03 / AVAILABILITY
          A = MTBF / (MTBF + MTTR)
          
      Uptime is a recovery-speed problem, not a never-fail problem.
